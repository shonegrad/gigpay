What you're looking at

“GigPay” is a pseudonym for a real product inside the LiveXchange / GigCX ecosystem. All names, amounts, rates and timelines here are fictional and generalized to respect confidentiality.

Interactions are live: confirming a payout updates balances and creates a trackable record. Exception actions resolve within the flow and are simulated.
